Kellgren-Lawrence (KL) Grading Scale

The system predicts one of five KL grades. These grades describe radiographic features associated with osteoarthritis.

Grade 0

Normal

Grade 1

Doubtful

Grade 2

Mild

Grade 3

Moderate

Grade 4

Severe
